How it works

Categories: tap one or more decades and genres. A song must match every selected category type; leave a category untouched to include all songs.

Clip length: choose 5, 10, 15, or 30 seconds. Audio fragments begin at a random point.

Two-player Quick Match: Player 1 hears the fragment and chooses, then passes the phone. Player 2 hears the same fragment and chooses independently.

Only add music you own, have permission to use, or that is royalty-free. Do not publish copyrighted commercial recordings in a public repository.
