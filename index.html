Bulk import adds up to 25 playable 30-second previews per search. Covers, karaoke, tributes, instrumentals, remixes and AI-generated tracks are filtered out. The game needs at least four matching songs.

How it works

Sources: Apple Music uses the iTunes Search API; Deezer uses its public search API. Both can provide 30-second previews.

Filters: decade and genre selections now strictly match imported songs. Quick-pack searches automatically tag imported songs with the pack’s decade and genre when the music service does not provide them.

Scores: after a round, enter a name and save it. Scores are stored in this browser with date and time.
