How it works

Deezer is the only connected music source. Search results come from live searches, not a fixed list.

Cover, kids’, piano and language exclusions are best-effort. Unknown album metadata is excluded when a corresponding filter is selected. Album dates may differ from original release dates.

Saved packs and score history are stored in this browser. Uploaded local files may need to be selected again after reopening the app.
